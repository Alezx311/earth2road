# Sources and attribution
- © OpenStreetMap contributors, https://www.openstreetmap.org/copyright, ODbL 1.0. Akademmistechko snapshot in `data/raw/akadem.osm`; checksum/timestamp in `data/build/akadem/sources.json`. West Kyiv: dated Geofabrik extract `data/raw/geofabrik/ukraine-260918.osm.pbf` (OSM data 2026-09-18, md5 in `config/west_kyiv.json`) cut by `tools/osm_extract.py` (pyosmium 4.3.1) into `data/raw/west_kyiv.osm`. Derived networks remain attributed OSM data.
- Overture Maps building footprints (buildings layer), ODbL 1.0, https://docs.overturemaps.org/ . Cached per map under `.cache/buildings/overture/`; shipped only when `building_enrichment.enabled: true` outputs them.
- Microsoft Building Footprints, CDLA-Permissive-2.0, https://www.microsoft.com/en-us/maps/building-footprints . Cached per map under `.cache/buildings/microsoft/`; shipped only when `building_enrichment.enabled: true` outputs them.
- Mapzen Terrain Tiles: https://registry.opendata.aws/terrain-tiles/ . Source-specific attribution: https://github.com/tilezen/joerd/blob/master/docs/attribution.md . For this area SRTM-derived terrain is approximate, not survey-grade road height.
- SUMO 1.27.1: EPL-2.0 / GPL-2.0-or-later, https://sumo.dlr.de/docs/ . Binaries installed separately, not committed.
- Godot 4.6: MIT, https://godotengine.org/license/ . Jolt bundled by Godot. Binary installed separately.
- Surface textures (asphalt, paving, grass, concrete, ground, gravel): ambientCG, CC0 1.0, https://ambientcg.com . Set IDs in config/textures.json; downloaded by tools/fetch_textures.py into game/assets/textures/ (not committed), per-file sha256 in game/assets/textures/manifest.json. Optional: without them the game uses flat colours.
- Vehicle models: Kenney Car Kit 3.1, CC0 1.0, https://kenney.nl/assets/car-kit . Pinned URL and sha256 in config/assets.json; tools/fetch_assets.py extracts 12 GLB + colormap into game/assets/cars/ (not committed). Bodies are rescaled to real-world sizes (config/vehicles.json).
- Vehicle physics: Godot Easy Vehicle Physics (GEVP), MIT, https://github.com/DAShoe1/Godot-Easy-Vehicle-Physics commit c392257f54f6ca537dc10bc5badad0c060f18982 — scripts vendored in game/addons/gevp/ with LICENSE (includes portions © 2021 Dechode, © 2024 Baron Wittman, MIT).
- Building meshes, facades, trees and road markings are procedurally generated in this repository. Facade windows, marking patterns, curb height and structure heights are assumptions, not surveyed.
- libsumo 1.27.1 (EPL-2.0 / GPL-2.0-or-later), pip wheel: SUMO in the bridge process instead of TraCI over a socket (tools/traffic.py falls back to traci if it is missing).
- Road works: Kyiv open data portal, dataset «Дані про перекриття та ремонти доріг у місті Києві» (Департамент транспортної інфраструктури КМДА), https://data.kyivcity.gov.ua/dataset/dani-pro-perekryttia-ta-remonty-dorih-u-misti-kyievi-dep-transport , GeoJSON from gisserver.kyivcity.gov.ua (URL in config/*.json → roadworks). Licence is not stated in the CKAN metadata; attribute the source. Snapshot in data/raw/roadworks.geojson (not committed); record its checksum in the local build audit. On 2026-09-19: 22 features, city-wide, 0 active in Akademmistechko.
- Traffic demand (tools/demand.py) is synthetic: derived from OSM buildings, points of interest and land use with assumed weights and an assumed hour-of-day mix. Kyiv detector counts (330 detectors, КМДА) are not published as open data (checked 2026-09-19); config/counts.json takes hand-entered observed counts with source and date.
